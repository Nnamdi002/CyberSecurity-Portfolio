## Overview

Today, I reflected on the importance of cybersecurity awareness and how everyday online activities can expose individuals to security threats.

## Key Areas Reviewed

**Password Security:** Using strong, unique passwords to protect online accounts.

**Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Adding an extra layer of protection beyond passwords.

**Phishing Awareness:** Recognising suspicious messages, links and requests for sensitive information.

**Software Updates:** Keeping applications and operating systems updated to address known vulnerabilities.

## Connection to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity awareness helps individuals recognise potential threats and make safer decisions when using technology.

While organisations implement technical security controls, users also play an important role in protecting information.

Continue exploring cybersecurity fundamentals and practise applying safe online habits.
