## Overview

Today, I continued my independent cybersecurity learning on TryHackMe, exploring the different types of computers and their roles in modern technology.

## What I Learned

I learned that computers are not limited to laptops and desktops. They exist in different forms and perform various functions.

| Computer Type | Function |
|---|---|
| Personal Computers | Used for everyday activities such as browsing, studying and working. |
| Servers | Provide services, information and resources to other computers over a network. |
| Internet of Things (IoT) Devices | Everyday devices that connect to networks, such as smart doorbells and smart appliances. |
| Embedded Systems | Computers built into larger devices to perform specific tasks. |

## Connection to Cybersecurity

Understanding different computer types is important because each system may have unique security requirements and vulnerabilities.

As more devices become connected to networks, the number of potential entry points for cyberattacks increases.

This knowledge provides a foundation for understanding how cybersecurity professionals identify and protect different types of systems.

## Key Takeaway

Cybersecurity begins with understanding the technology we want to protect. Recognising different computer types helps me appreciate why security measures must be adapted to different devices and environments.
